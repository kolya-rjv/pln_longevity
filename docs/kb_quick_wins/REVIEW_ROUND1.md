# Review round 1 — the medication reader and the phase-1 commits

An adversarial workflow reviewed commits `732d82a`..`0f69c51` (items #2, #1, #4, #5, #9, #3 of `REPORT.md`): six red-team lenses on the new medication reader, five review lenses on the commits, and an independent skeptic for every finding (it re-ran the finding and tried to refute it).

**12 reviewers → 115 findings → 82 confirmed by a skeptic, 33 refuted or not reproduced → 40 root causes** (below). About 1.3 million distinct reader inputs were tried; almost every confirmed finding was a way the first version of the medication reader read a drug the person does not take. The reader was redesigned to be strict rather than patched (`pln_chat/core/patient_medications.py` header); every input the review confirmed is pinned in `tests/test_patient_medications.py` (`NEVER_CURRENT`, 220+ inputs; `HOSTILE`, the stalls).
